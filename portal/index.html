About the System

🏛️ Official Gazette Alignment

The system verifies eligibility directly against official Government Resolutions (GRs), the Maharashtra Land Revenue Code, and the Registration of Births & Deaths Act. All decisions cite specific statutory sections for full legal transparency.

👮 Officer Oversight & Audit

Applications requiring additional scrutiny are seamlessly routed to designated revenue officers with detailed document summaries and compliance logs. Officers hold complete administrative authority on all certificate decisions.
